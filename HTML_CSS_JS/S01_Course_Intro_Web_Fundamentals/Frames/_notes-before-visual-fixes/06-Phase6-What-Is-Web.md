## ⏱️ 32:02 – 32:58 · 🧩 The Definition ⭐

🔑 **Key points**
- 📖 **Web is a portion of the internet with restricted access.**
- 🧩 It is **part of** the internet, but **restricted** in access.

🧠 **Hook:** *Web = internet slice + restrictions.*

---

## ⏱️ 32:58 – 34:12 · 🧩 Netflix Shows the Restrictions 🎬

🔑 **Key points**
| 💰 Plan (per month, as said) | 🔒 What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| ₹199 | **1 device** only |
| ~₹400 | **4 devices** |
| ~₹600 – ₹800 | **4K** quality |

- 🔒 You are restricted by **number of devices**, **amount of content**, and even **video quality**.
- 📌 *"This is the nature of web."*

---

## ⏱️ 34:12 – 35:01 · 🧩 Restricted by Region Too 🌍

🔑 **Key points**
- 🇺🇸 ≠ 🇮🇳 **Netflix US is different from Netflix India.** Some US titles aren't available here because the country's government restricts them by its laws.
- 🎯 *"You are not using the internet directly. You are using the **web**."*
```
  🌐 INTERNET
   └── 🕸️ WEB  (a portion, with restricted access)
         ├─ 👤 WHO can access        (subscribers only)
         ├─ 📊 HOW MUCH they access  (1 vs 4 devices · HD vs 4K)
         └─ 🎬 WHAT they access      (Netflix India ≠ Netflix US)
```

---

## ⏱️ 35:01 – 35:30 · 🧩 Your Website Is Restricted by You

🔑 **Key points**
- 🛠️ When **you** build a website, it has information, and **you** decide:
```
  👤 who can watch   ·   📊 how much they can watch   ·   🎬 what they can watch
```
- 🔒 That is why the web is **restricted in nature**.

---

## ⏱️ 35:30 – 36:00 · 🧩 Tim Berners-Lee: Father of the Web 👨‍🔬

🔑 **Key points**
- 💡 **Tim Berners-Lee** introduced, designed and developed the concept of the **web**.
- 🏷️ Called the **Father of the Web**.
- 📄 He also **invented HTML**.

---

## ⏱️ 36:00 – 36:30 · 🧩 W3C: Who Maintains the Web 🏛️

🔑 **Key points**
- 🏛️ **W3C = World Wide Web Consortium** maintains the **web standards**.
- 📏 If you build a website, you must follow **W3C's rules**.
- ⚠️ *"In real time, you cannot build a website just with what you know about it. You have to build as per rules."*
```
  👨‍🔬 Tim Berners-Lee ──created──► 🕸️ WEB + 📄 HTML
                                       │
                         🏛️ W3C ──maintains standards──► 📏 rules every website follows
```

🧠 **Hook:** *Tim built it; W3C keeps the rulebook.*
