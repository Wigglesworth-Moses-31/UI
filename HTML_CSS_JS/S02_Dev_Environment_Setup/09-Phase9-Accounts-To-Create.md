## ⏱️ 50:21 – 51:57 · 🧩 Why Magnific? Copyright ⚠️

🔑 **Key points**
- 🖼️ Provides **icons, images, videos** used by developers in web design. It also has **AI integration**.
- 🚫 In web development you **can't Google, download a photo and use it**: images may have **copyright** issues → your website or channel can be **banned / blocked**.
- 🔍 Demo: search **"travel banner"** (for a travel website) → travel banners appear.
- 💰 Some are **free**, some **paid**; a **symbol** on an item marks it as **paid**. Free ones you can download and use.
- 🎬 **The paid symbol (51:35):** a small **crown badge** in the bottom-left corner of the thumbnail. The cursor is beside one just after he says "this symbol"; thumbnails without the crown are the free ones.
- 🎬 **The demo as seen (51:03–51:49):**

```text
https://www.magnific.com                    home page: "The creative platform to
                                            direct your best work"
search box: Travel Banner                   suggestions: Search images of / videos of /
                                            icons of "travel banner", Create an image of …
https://www.magnific.com/search?format=search&term=travel+banner
result page: travel banner · 23.1m results
filters:     All images · License · AI-generated · Orientation · Color · People
```

- 🎬 Right after "this is not just images" he opens the **All images** filter (51:49). It lists: Vectors, Illustrations, Photos, PSD, Templates, Mockups, Videos, Footage, Motion graphics, Video templates, Icons, 3D Models, Fonts.
- 🎬 Hovering a result shows a **Use** button and a **download** icon on it (51:39).

🧠 **Hook:** *Never Google-and-grab images; use a licensed source.*
